Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Metrics: Dota Performance ("Metrics" or "the App") is an on-device companion for viewing and analyzing public Dota 2 data. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. No Metrics Account

The App does not require or provide a Metrics account. You do not submit a password, payment information, or Dota 2 credentials to the App.

2. Public OpenDota Data

When you search for a player or request match information, the App sends the relevant search text, public account ID, or match ID directly to OpenDota so OpenDota can return the requested public data. Metrics does not operate an intermediary server and does not receive a copy of those requests.

The returned data can include public player profiles, match history, heroes, and match statistics. Availability depends on the player's Dota 2 and OpenDota privacy settings. OpenDota handles requests under its own policies.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores user-facing records and preferences on your device, including:

  • Your selected player and favorite players
  • Recently viewed public player and match information
  • Preferences and app settings
  • Cached OpenDota responses used for faster loading and offline viewing

You can clear cached data from Settings. Removing the App also removes its locally stored data, subject to standard iOS backup behavior.

4. Share Cards

Share cards are generated on your device. Metrics does not upload them. A card leaves the App only when you choose a destination through the iOS share sheet.

5. No Analytics, Advertising, or Tracking

Metrics does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or behavioral profiling. We do not sell personal information.

6. Developer Access

Because Metrics has no developer-operated account system, analytics service, or backend, the developer does not receive or maintain a database of your App activity.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Public Dota 2 data is provided by OpenDota and is subject to the source account's visibility settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the App's behavior changes. The effective date above will be updated when a new policy is posted.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email stridequests.support@gmail.com.
