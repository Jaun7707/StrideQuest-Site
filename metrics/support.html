Metrics Support

Metrics uses public OpenDota data. The answers below cover the most common setup and data-availability questions.

Contact

Email stridequests.support@gmail.com and include your public account ID, iOS version, and a brief description of what happened. Never send a Steam or Dota 2 password.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does searching my Dota name not find me?

Name search depends on OpenDota's public search index and may be incomplete. Enter your numeric account ID directly. For example, the account Jaun7707 uses 60271617.

Where can I find my account ID?

Open your player page on OpenDota or Dotabuff and copy the number in the page address. Metrics accepts the unformatted numeric account ID.

Why are matches or profile details missing?

Metrics can show only data available through OpenDota. Private profiles, disabled public match data, newly played matches that have not been parsed, and temporary OpenDota service issues can limit results.

How do I refresh stale data?

Use the refresh control on the relevant screen. If a cached response is still stale, open Settings and clear the endpoint cache, then retry while connected to the internet.

Does Metrics need my Steam or Dota 2 login?

No. Metrics is keyless and account-free. It requests public data directly from OpenDota and never asks for your Steam or Dota 2 password.

What data does Metrics collect?

Metrics has no analytics, ads, tracking, or developer backend. Favorites, settings, and cached public responses stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
